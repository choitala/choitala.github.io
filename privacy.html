Privacy policy

This app is used only by its owner, to upload the owner's own video files to the owner's own Google Drive folders and to read those files' Drive links.

  • It runs on the owner's own computers through rclone. There is no server and no other users.
  • Google Drive access is used only to create folders, upload files and list them in the owner's Drive.
  • No data is collected, stored elsewhere, sold or shared with anyone.
